About us

From Rotterdam to Arnhem.

Our logo connects two cities: the Erasmus Bridge stands for Rotterdam, where Wilfred is from, and the tower of St. Eusebius' Church for Arnhem, where Bo is from. Two landmarks that have stood firm for years. That is what we want to be for our tenants.

Vastor works closely with Van Zoest Vastgoed. We put that experience in buying, renovating and letting property to use in our management every day.
